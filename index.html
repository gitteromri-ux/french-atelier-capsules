Offer

The offer

Most buyers take the $229 package ($79 × 3 months); there is also an option of about $600. Think of it as $79–$99 a month for 3 months: a light, easy purchase. The $5,000 is the whole French Atelier budget.

Path. a light, easy purchase: $613 → $408 → $327 → $258 a paid sale. Cost base: eTeacher's own past campaign data ($1.92 a landing page view); add to cart → purchase 50% ASSUMPTION.

Control

Gates and safeguards

  1. Gate 0

    Before any spend: page shows the new prices, one test purchase shows in Meta and the CRM.

  2. Day 5

    Day 5 (plan: 5 purchases, 13 adds to cart): at least 2 purchases and 7 adds to cart, or stop.

  3. Gate B

    Gate B: cost per purchase at or under $613 (1.5 times the week-2 plan) and cost per landing page view at or under $2.87. Otherwise cold budget drops to $150 a day.

  4. Week 3 rule

    Continuation rule: cost per purchase at or under $375, heading to about $300. If not, Capsules stops. Add to cart → purchase at 50% or more.

Kill rules

An ad spending $300 with 0 adds to cart is paused. An ad set spending 2 times target with 0 checkouts is paused. Ceilings: cost per landing page view above $16 for 3 days; frequency above 3 cold or 5 warm in 7 days.

Caps and stop-loss

Daily budgets $700, so a week is $4,900, under the $5,000 cap. Budget changes at most 20%, once per 48 hours. A week above 2.5 times target cost per purchase: next week warm audiences only; two such weeks: all ads stop.

Who decides

Omri, at the weekly go / no-go meeting. Gita proposes each pause or change; nothing changes in Meta without Omri's go.

Sum check and funnel used

Sum check. Every week = its 7 days; every day $700. Spend $4,900 + $4,900 + $4,900 + $4,900 = $19,600. Purchases 8 + 12 + 15 + 19 = 54. Organic 1 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 8. All sales 9 + 14 + 17 + 22 = 62. Views 2,558 + 2,559 + 2,558 + 2,559 = 10,234. Adds to cart 20 + 24 + 30 + 38 = 112. Checkouts 18 + 22 + 28 + 34 = 102.

Cost base: eTeacher's own past campaign data: 15.4 landing page views per 1,000 impressions, 88.3% of clicks load the page. Add to cart → checkout 91.2% (eTeacher's past campaign data). Add to cart → purchase 50% ASSUMPTION. Paid sales per week: 8 → 12 → 15 → 19; Capsules has 0 purchases on days 1–2 and 1 on day 3. Daily sales are whole numbers, rounded on the running total. Organic 12.5%: organic = paid ÷ 7, rounded on the running total by week.

Reference

Terms

CPM
Cost per 1,000 impressions (times an ad is shown).
Landing page view
A click that fully loaded the sales page.
Checkout
The payment page opened.
Paid, blended
Paid = sales from ads; blended = all sales including organic (email, social, word of mouth), 12.5%.
ESTIMATE
Projection.
ASSUMPTION
Value chosen where no data exists, for Omri to confirm.
