Offer

The offer

Price per buyer is about $270 to $300 ($99 × 3). The sales team upgrades about 1 in 8 buyers in the same month, about 2 to 3 upgrades on this budget. The $6,000 is the whole test budget: $300 a day for 20 days.

Note. Good and bad days happen. Each range is the average for its 5-day block. ESTIMATE

Control

Gates and safeguards

  1. Gate 0

    Before any spend: page shows the new prices, one test purchase shows in Meta and the CRM.

  2. Days 1–5

    0 to 1 sales, $1,500 or more per sale. Stop if fewer than 6 add to carts.

  3. Days 6–10

    3 to 5 sales, $300 to $500 per sale. Stop if fewer than 3 sales.

  4. Days 11–15

    About 7 sales, about $220 per sale. Stop if a sale costs more than $350. Days 16–20: 8 to 9 sales, $170 to $185 per sale.

Stop rules

Each 5-day block has one stop rule (above). If it trips, the test stops.

Cap

$300 a day for 20 days, $6,000 in total, never exceeded.

Who decides

Omri, at the end of each 5-day block. Gita proposes each pause or change; nothing changes in Meta without Omri's go.
